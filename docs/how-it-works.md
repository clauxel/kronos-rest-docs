# How It Works - Kronos AI

Kronos AI helps teams check OHLCV readiness, choose forecast lanes, review volatility scenarios, and connect open-source Kronos research to a managed workflow.

## Prepare candle data

Confirm symbol, market, interval, OHLC columns, optional volume or amount, timestamp order, and missing values.

## Choose forecast horizon

Select whether the task is short-horizon movement, volatility review, synthetic-data sanity check, or research planning.

## Review model assumptions

Compare data shape, market regime, evaluation gate, and risk-control expectations.

## Move into workspace

Use the paid plan when the team needs repeatable reviews, saved outputs, and collaboration.
