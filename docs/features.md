# Features - Kronos AI

Kronos AI helps teams check OHLCV readiness, choose forecast lanes, review volatility scenarios, and connect open-source Kronos research to a managed workflow.

## OHLCV readiness check

Validate open, high, low, close, volume, amount, timestamp, and horizon assumptions before model review.

## K-line forecast workflow

Treat financial candles as structured sequences for scenario review instead of a generic time-series demo.

## Volatility and regime notes

Flag market regimes, missing volume, horizon mismatch, and review risks before production use.

## Open-source repo bridge

Point researchers to the upstream Kronos repo while explaining what a managed workspace adds.

## Pricing and checkout clarity

Keep the Pro annual path and paid workspace value visible for users comparing commercial options.

## Risk-control framing

Encourage backtests, walk-forward validation, slippage assumptions, and human review.

## Pricing and Support

Plans cover forecast workspace access, Pro annual workflows, data-readiness checks, scenario review, and team support.
