# Guides - Kronos AI

Kronos AI helps teams check OHLCV readiness, choose forecast lanes, review volatility scenarios, and connect open-source Kronos research to a managed workflow.

## Prepare OHLCV data

Sort timestamps, preserve interval consistency, include volume when available, and separate training from evaluation periods.

## Evaluate a forecast safely

Use backtests, walk-forward validation, slippage, drawdown review, and human oversight before action.

## Avoid Kronos name confusion

Use Kronos AI or K-line forecasting labels when you mean this product, not unrelated HR or workforce software.
